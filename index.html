What she does

She is not waiting politely in a menu.

Biskit gets hungry, bored and grubby on her own schedule, fidgets when you go quiet, and notices how long you were gone.

She repeats you

Say something and Biskit says it back, higher and sillier. The recording never leaves your device.

She lives while you're away

Close the tab and time still passes. Come back and she will tell you exactly what she got up to — and what she needs.

She wants a snack

Fish, milk, steak, cake, sushi. Earn coins in the mini-games to pay for the good stuff, then watch her demolish it.

Two mini-games

Catch falling fish, or play Copycat — she taps out a tune and you tap it back, one step longer every round.

She has a hat problem

Ten hats, unlocked as you level up. She will wear a crown to bed and nobody can stop her.

She is yours to keep

One tap gives you a backup code for your whole pet. Change browser, change phone — paste it in and she is back.

Coming soon

Biskit on your home screen

The iOS and Android apps are on the way, with notifications for when she genuinely needs something. Until then the browser version is the whole game — no cut-down demo, no account, nothing missing.
