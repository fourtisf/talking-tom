# Biskit — copy deck

English, professional. Every number and claim below is taken from the code, not
from ambition: 5 rooms, 10 hats, 6 outfits, 5 rugs, 16 awards, 3 daily tasks,
2 mini-games, rewarded video only, notifications capped at 2 a day with a
22:00–08:00 quiet window. Where a line could not be verified it is not here.

---

## 1. Voice

Biskit's voice is **dry, warm and specific**. It is the voice already on the
landing page and in her speech bubbles, and marketing that shouts breaks it.

**Do**
- Understate. "She has opinions about it" beats "AMAZING personality!"
- Be concrete. "Ten hats" beats "tons of customisation".
- Let her be slightly rude. She hisses. That is the joke.
- Short sentences. One idea each.

**Don't**
- No exclamation marks in headlines. One per page of body copy, maximum.
- No "immersive", "engaging experience", "your new best friend", "unleash".
- No feature lists as adjectives — count the thing instead.
- Never name a competitor, and never imply a licence or endorsement.

**Person and tense.** Biskit is *she*. The player is *you*. Present tense.

## 7. Notifications

Two a day maximum, never between 22:00 and 08:00. Each one is scheduled for the
moment a real stat crosses its threshold, so it has to be true when it lands —
which is why none of these says "come back!".

There are exactly **five** triggers in the code — hunger, energy, fun, clean and
relief — and each takes a title and a body. Drop-in replacements for
`notify.*.title` / `notify.*.body` in `src/i18n/en.ts`:

| Trigger | Title | Body |
|---|---|---|
| Hunger | {pet} is getting hungry 🐟 | The bowl has been empty for a while now. |
| Energy | {pet} is worn out 💤 | She has yawned four times. Bed? |
| Fun | {pet} is bored 🎈 | Bored enough to knock something off a shelf. |
| Clean | {pet} needs a scrub 🫧 | Bath time, and she will not agree. |
| Relief | {pet} needs the litter tray | She is doing the little dance. |

*Not wired, do not write for it yet:* there is no "you have been away a day"
notification and no streak reminder. The daily-login ladder exists (50 coins
rising to 300 on day 7) but nothing pushes it.

## 9. Words we do not use

- Any competitor's product or character name, in copy, keywords or hashtags.
- "Tamagotchi" — someone's trademark, not a category.
- Claims we cannot show: "millions of players", "award-winning", "#1".
- "AI" — nothing here is a language model, and saying so would be a lie.
- "Free" without the qualifier where a purchase exists. It is free to play; say
  that.
